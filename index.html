Pick a trap (keys 1-9) and tap a tile. The ground is flat. Building blocks cost $1 and can go anywhere. One block is a step that challengers climb slowly. Stack two or more and it becomes a wall: they stop and smash it down, bunching up in front of it. Tap a block to take it back for $1. Spikes, mines, flames and crushers sit on the ground or on top of a stack; everything else floats anywhere. Tap a built trap to sell it for half of what you paid. Swipe the board sideways to see the course. Challengers read the traps ahead: they bunch up (blue dot) against turrets and Tesla coils, and spread out (orange dot) against blasts, flames, spikes and saws. Orange hats sprint in bursts, grey helmets hesitate near traps, and pink hats hop over floor spikes. Every kill pays more as your total grows, and chaining kills adds a streak bonus.
